Questions

Good to know.

Does Fiora approve loans?

No. Fiora is a lead-generation platform. Eligibility, approval, pricing and final terms are decided by the relevant lender.

What happens after I submit?

Your enquiry is recorded and may be reviewed for relevant loan options. You may be contacted using the details you provide and consent to.

Is every loan type available to everyone?

No. Availability and eligibility vary by lender, product, location, income profile and other criteria.

Can I change my details later?

If you need help with a submitted enquiry or your data, use the contact details and privacy process provided by the platform.
