# DSA / Lending Partner Workflow

Credora is positioned as a lead-generation platform, not a lender.

Suggested operating flow:
1. Customer submits an enquiry with consent.
2. Lead appears in the admin dashboard.
3. Team verifies basic details and eligibility information.
4. Lead is assigned to an appropriate DSA/lending partner under a written commercial arrangement.
5. Partner updates progress outside/alongside the internal status workflow.
6. Keep evidence of lead source, consent, partner assignment, duplicate/rejection rules, and payout terms.

Before sharing customer data with a partner, make sure the privacy notice and consent wording cover the relevant sharing purpose and that the partner agreement is in place.

Commercial terms should explicitly define whether payout is based on sanctioned or disbursed amount, payment trigger, duplicate rules, rejected cases, clawbacks, and settlement timing.
