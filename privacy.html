Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

This page is a starter policy for the demo platform. Before public launch, the business should have this document reviewed and adapted to its actual data flows, partners, retention periods and applicable law.

Information we collect

When you submit a loan enquiry, we may collect your name, mobile number, email, city, loan requirement, income, employment details, existing EMI information and the CIBIL range you choose to provide.

How we use information

We use submitted information to process your enquiry, contact you about relevant loan options, maintain enquiry records and, where you have consented, share relevant enquiry information with appropriate financial or lending partners.

Consent

You can choose whether to submit an enquiry. The website should clearly identify the purposes for which information is collected and shared. Consent records are retained with the enquiry.

Security and retention

Reasonable technical and organisational measures should be used to protect enquiry information. Retention and deletion periods should be defined before production launch.
