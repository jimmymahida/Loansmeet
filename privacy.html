Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LOANSMEET may collect, use, store and share information submitted through its website and loan-assistance services. The operating legal entity, verified contact details, grievance details and retention schedule must be inserted before public launch.

Information we collect

When you submit a loan enquiry, we may collect your name, mobile number, email, city, loan requirement, income, employment details, existing EMI information and the CIBIL range you choose to provide.

How we use information

We use submitted information to process your enquiry, contact you about relevant loan options, maintain enquiry records and, where you have consented, share relevant enquiry information with appropriate financial or lending partners.

Consent

You can choose whether to submit an enquiry. The website should clearly identify the purposes for which information is collected and shared. Consent records are retained with the enquiry.

Security and retention

We use access controls, authentication, audit logging, rate limiting, input validation and protected storage appropriate to the service. Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the stated purposes or as required by applicable law and contractual obligations.

Contact

For privacy requests, data-access/deletion requests or complaints, use the verified support and grievance contact published on this website. The final public version must include the operating legal entity name, address and official contact channels.
